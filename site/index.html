How these numbers are worked out
  • Source: D365 Finance tables in Databricks (ledger journal header and lines), journal name GJV, all legal entities. Only lines dated 1 January 2026 or later are included, whatever the From date says.
  • Dates are the line transaction date. D365 does not fill the journal header's created date in this data, so the journal is placed by its line dates.
  • Created by is the D365 user on the journal header.
  • Posting type comes from the posted general ledger entry for the same voucher and account. Customer, vendor and bank lines show the balance posting type D365 uses for them. Journals not yet posted show "Not posted".
  • Account names: GL accounts use the main account name, customers and vendors use their party name in the line's company.
  • Amounts are in each line's transaction currency, so totals are only added within one currency.
  • Export to Excel saves Summary, Journals and Lines sheets with every journal and line that matches the filters.
  • The figures are a snapshot taken by the scheduled refresh (the time is at the top of the page), not a live query. Each refresh checks every month and legal entity against totals Databricks computes separately and is not published if they disagree.
  • Only people on the access list can open this report.
